This website was created as a college project to present Formula 1 in an interactive and visually engaging format. It demonstrates responsive web design, navigation, animations, cards, tables and multi-page structure.

The information on this demo site is for educational purposes. Data such as live standings and race schedules should be checked against official sources before being treated as current.
